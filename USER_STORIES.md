# Whetmark — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Field technician or engineer

- As a field technician, I want to get an answer to a specific procedural question in under two minutes while I am standing at the asset, so that I do not either guess or abandon the job.
- As a field technician, I want to see my own currency board showing what I hold, what expires when, and what evidence is missing, so that I find out about a lapse months in advance rather than at the depot gate on a Monday morning.
- As a field technician, I want to book my own reassessment against real assessor availability, so that keeping my authorisations live is something I can act on rather than wait for.
- As a field technician whose authorisation has lapsed on paper, I want to contest that determination by pointing to the work I have actually been doing, so that a gap in the evidence feed does not cost me shifts I am competent to work.

### Resource scheduler or team leader

- As a resource scheduler, I want tomorrow's roster to show currency-aware coverage per capability, so that I discover an uncovered authorisation the day before rather than at hand-over.
- As a team leader, I want to see which single reassessment would unlock the most otherwise-unfillable shifts across my depot, so that I spend scarce assessor time where it buys the most operational capacity.
- As a team leader who has only an uncertified person available for urgent work, I want to raise a time-boxed override with explicit compensating controls and a named authoriser, so that the exception is governed and visible instead of being a quiet decision I carry personally.

### Competence assessor or verifier

- As an assessor, I want my worklist ordered by operational exposure rather than by expiry date, so that I clear the lapses that are actually stopping work first.
- As an assessor, I want to capture observation evidence against the published standard at the point of assessment, so that the record is defensible later without my having to reconstruct it from memory.
- As an assessor, I want to decline an assessment where I am not qualified in that capability or not sufficiently independent of the person, so that the register cannot be populated with attestations that would not survive scrutiny.

### Capability designer

- As a capability designer, I want time-to-proficiency reported per entry route, so that I can retire interventions that consume budget without moving the only number operations cares about.
- As a capability designer, I want to see which point-of-work questions recur across a team, so that I can tell the difference between a knowledge gap I should address with learning and a process or tooling defect I should escalate.
- As a capability designer, I want to set and version the decay basis for each capability with a stated rationale, so that an expiry interval is a reviewable professional judgement rather than an inherited default nobody can explain.

### Compliance officer and employee representative

- As a compliance officer, I want an evidence pack for a single individual and a single capability determination, assembled in one place, so that I can answer a regulator or a tribunal without a document hunt across three systems.
- As an employee representative, I want the adverse impact report on assessment and lapse rates broken down by working pattern and leave history, so that I can challenge a decay basis that penalises part-time members and returners before it becomes custom and practice.
- As a governance administrator, I want to control who may publish a decay basis, who may authorise a deployment override, and how long observation evidence is retained, so that the two most consequential powers in the system are held by named, auditable roles.
